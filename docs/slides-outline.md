1. **Title.** Vocalis: teach it to learn it. Team name, members, one-line promise: "An AI student that questions you until your gaps show." Theme: AI for Education.
2. **The problem and the user.** Student revising alone from their own notes. Re-reading feels like learning but is not (illusion of explanatory depth; Dunlosky 2013: re-reading and highlighting = low utility, practice testing and self-explanation = better).
   Add your classmate poll result here. One big number if you have it.
3. **The idea.** The Feynman technique in four steps (choose, teach, find gaps, review) beside "what Vocalis automates": the listener who asks good questions and finds the gaps.
4. **How it works.** One flow picture: upload -> topics -> AI asks 5 questions (voice / text / whiteboard) -> score + gap report -> re-upload gaps. Screenshot of the chat with the orb.
5. **What makes it different.** Three screenshots: (a) whiteboard that the AI understands, with a detailed AI-drawn diagram to label; (b) the report page with the four scores and gap cards; (c) Kannada revision notes / Word report.
6. **Technology and responsible AI.** Flask, SQLite, Gemini (structured JSON, vision, Google Search grounding). Score computed in code. Notes stay private to the user's account. Accessibility: voice or typing, large text, time limits optional, multilingual.
7. **Evidence it works.** Automated tests (logic, API, extraction, browser flow), a live-run screenshot, and any pilot result: "N students tried it, M found a gap they had not noticed". Be honest about what is tested.
