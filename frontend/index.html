About Vocalis

If you can teach it,
you know it.

Vocalis is a study partner built on the Feynman technique. You explain a topic out loud to a curious AI student, it asks questions until the gaps in your understanding show, and then it hands you a clear report of exactly what to revise.

The Feynman technique

The technique is named after the physicist Richard Feynman, who was famous for explaining difficult ideas in plain words. The idea is simple: you do not really understand something until you can explain it simply. It works in four steps.

1

Choose a concept

Pick one idea you want to learn, and write down everything you know about it.

2

Teach it simply

Explain it as if to a beginner, using your own words and no jargon you cannot unpack.

3

Find the gaps

Notice where you stumble, go vague or get something wrong. Those are the gaps in your understanding.

4

Review and simplify

Go back to your notes, fix each gap, then explain it again, shorter and clearer.

The hard part is step 3. When you explain to yourself, it is easy to skip over what you do not know. A listener who asks good questions makes the gaps impossible to hide. That is the part Vocalis automates.

How Vocalis puts each step into practice

1 · Choose a conceptUpload and pick a topic
  • Upload your own notes: PDF, PowerPoint, Word, text, Markdown or a photo.
  • Long files are split into numbered subtopics with clear headings, and you choose the one to be tested on.
2 · Teach it simplyExplain to a curious AI
  • The AI opens the conversation and asks exactly five questions, each one following your last answer.
  • Answer by typing, speaking, or drawing and labelling on the whiteboard, including diagrams the AI draws for you to label.
  • Optional time limit per question and optional read-aloud keep it feeling like a real viva.
3 · Find the gapsScores, gaps and fact-check
  • An examiner AI scores accuracy, completeness, consistency and clarity (each out of 10) using only your own notes, then gives an overall score out of 100.
  • Every wrong or missing idea becomes a gap with what you said, the correct explanation, why it matters and a question to test yourself.
  • A web-search fact-check flags anything in your notes or your answers that may be wrong.
4 · Review and simplifyNotes, reports and re-testing
  • Revision notes focus only on the parts you struggled with, and can be translated into Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu or Malayalam.
  • Download a detailed gap report and a full session report as Word documents.
  • Upload the gap report again and Vocalis tests you on exactly those gaps. Repeat until the gaps close.
